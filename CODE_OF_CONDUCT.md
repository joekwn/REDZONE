# Code of Conduct

REDZONE follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give and accept constructive
feedback, and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults and
personal attacks are not accepted.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through the email on
their GitHub profile, or open a private security advisory if it involves a safety issue.
Reports are handled confidentially.
