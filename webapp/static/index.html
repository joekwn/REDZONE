About the data

Sources

  • MODIS Collection 6.1 active fires (MCD14DL), Terra + Aqua, ~1 km pixels — NASA FIRMS.
  • VIIRS 375 m active fires, NOAA-21 — NASA FIRMS.
  • Archive (standard) data where available, near-real-time (NRT) data for the latest months. Past MODIS days come from MODIS_SP (standard quality); past VIIRS NOAA-21 days are only available from FIRMS as NRT.

What we did to the data

  • Harmonized both products into one format; no duplicates were found; nothing was deleted.
  • "Look up older data" asks NASA FIRMS for one area and up to 10 days, applies the same rules, and shows the result without saving it.
  • These rules were developed and tested on the one-year core set (1 Nov 2025 to 1 Nov 2026). Days added later through the FIRMS API (live updates and past-data collection) go through the same rules automatically, but have not been checked by hand as thoroughly.
  • Flagged 8 negative-FRP and 7,922 zero-FRP detections; their FRP is excluded from totals.
  • Flagged 391 polar detections (e.g. Mount Erebus volcano, Antarctica); hidden by default.
  • NRT records have no "type" field (NASA does not provide it), so volcanoes and industrial heat sources cannot be filtered out for the latest months.
